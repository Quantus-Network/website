# Quantum Canary Website Bios.

# Christopher Smith

### Recommended role line

Founder and CEO, Quantus

### Website bio

Christopher Smith is Editor-in-Chief of Quantum Canary and founder and CEO of Quantus. A technology entrepreneur working across artificial intelligence, blockchain and quantum computing, he has spent more than a decade building tools for decentralized systems. His earlier ventures include BitMesh, Lunyr and FactoryDAO, and he contributed to early Bitcoin software and privacy tooling. Chris is the author of the Quantus whitepaper and host of the QDay podcast, where he speaks with researchers and builders about quantum computing, cryptography and the future of digital money.

# Joseph Mattia

### Recommended role line

Co-founder and COO, Quantus 

### Website bio

Joseph Mattia is co-founder and COO of Quantus, where he leads operations and helps turn post-quantum research into product execution and community programs. He has represented Quantus at QDay events and co-hosted early episodes of the QDay podcast, bringing an operator's perspective to questions about blockchain security, adoption and coordination. Joe focuses on the practical choices builders, institutions and asset holders must make before quantum risk becomes an emergency.

### Short card version

Quantus co-founder and COO focused on the operational, commercial and community choices required for a post-quantum financial system.

# Jonathan Angle

Communications Director, Quantus

Jonathan “Jangle” Angle is Director of Communications at Quantus, the post-quantum blockchain.
